# Custom 3D Models Directory

Drop your downloaded 3D model files here!
Supported formats:
- **Wavefront OBJ** (`.obj`)
- **glTF 2.0 / GLB** (`.gltf`, `.glb`)

### How to use in Surreal 3D Studio:
1. Copy or download any `.obj` or `.gltf` model into this directory (`assets/custom_models/`) or into `assets/models/`.
2. In Surreal Studio, press `[TAB]` to enter World Editor Mode.
3. Press `[L]` (or click `[L] Load & Place Model`) to open the **3D Model Folder Importer & Browser**.
4. Press `[TAB]` to switch to the `custom_models` folder, or press `[F5]` to rescan.
5. Click on your model or press its assigned number key `[1]–[9]`.
6. Position the model with your mouse over the ground or platforms!
7. Left-click to place the model into your level!
